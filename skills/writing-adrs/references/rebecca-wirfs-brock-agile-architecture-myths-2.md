# Rebecca Wirfs-Brock — Agile Architecture Myths #2

- Author: Rebecca Wirfs-Brock; published 2011-01-18
- Accessed: 2026-10-03 (public article HTML)
- This is a paraphrased research note, **not a copy of the article**.

## Guidance

Do not apply the "last responsible moment" mechanically. Delaying a choice can constrain other work or cause a decision to emerge accidentally through local implementation. Early architecture decisions may have positive impact if vetted and revisited as necessary. Her framing favors making choices at the *most responsible moment*, when they help the team, rather than always at the latest possible moment.

## Application to the skill

Ask why the decision is needed now and what uncertainty can still be tested. Record assumptions so a later team can tell when to reconsider the choice. This is decision-timing guidance, **not** an ADR template.
