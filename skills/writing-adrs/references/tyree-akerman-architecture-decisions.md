## Relevance to this skill

Malan says Tyree and Akerman's earlier decision template records alternatives considered but ruled out. She calls that valuable alongside the lighter Nygard template. The skill therefore asks for a brief, fair account of credible rejected alternatives where it explains why the chosen option fits. It does **not** import the full template or claim that Tyree and Akerman prescribe the skill's exact wording.

Malan also mentions Olaf Zimmermann's earlier template in the same context; no standalone primary-source note is included here because that work was not directly verified.
