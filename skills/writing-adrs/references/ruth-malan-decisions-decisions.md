## Ideas relevant to ADRs

Malan contrasts delaying decisions with making early, consequential decisions when they help others move forward. She cites Rebecca Wirfs-Brock's argument that early high-impact choices are not automatically wasteful: vet them and revisit them if needed. Architecture decisions establish context for subsequent decisions and narrow the space of options; doing that deliberately can reduce ambiguity. Probe and test those choices rather than assuming they are final forever.

## Application to the skill

Record why the choice was appropriate *at the time*, including assumptions and forces. Do not treat the resulting record as a timeless mandate. If new evidence changes the choice, explicitly supersede it while retaining the original rationale.
